# LoqMart state story publishing plan

The editorial brief calls for 28 original state stories, delivered in seven batches of four to preserve accuracy and avoid truncation. The first batch is now available in the Craft Chronicles and through the corresponding state pages and map popovers.

## Batch 1 — published in code

1. Andhra Pradesh — Kalamkari, Kondapalli toys, handlooms and the coast
2. Arunachal Pradesh — mountain communities, bamboo knowledge and living traditions
3. Assam — Ahom history, Muga/Eri/pat silk, tea and bamboo
4. Bihar — Nalanda, Madhubani painting, trade history and Bhagalpuri silk

The user-provided regional images have descriptive filenames and are used on matching state experiences: Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Haryana, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Rajasthan, and West Bengal. Two additional images provide alternate map-preview art for Jammu and Kashmir and Rajasthan. These are illustrative, AI-generated scenes, not documentary photographs of actual workshops or sellers. Product listing images remain separate from editorial artwork.

The first four article pages include unique metadata, regional greeting, structured H2 sections, FAQs, category links, cross-story links, and seller-discovery CTAs. They intentionally avoid unsupported GI certification claims and do not imply that specific artisans are already LoqMart sellers.
